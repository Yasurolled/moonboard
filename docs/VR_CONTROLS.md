# MoonBoard VR controls

## Requirements

VR mode supports Android 8.0 (API 26) and newer. Use a Cardboard-compatible
viewer and enable VR mode in the app preferences.

## Head-centered reticle

A small crosshair at the center of each eye's view indicates the current gaze
direction. Use it to aim at the desktop or camera PiP title bar.

## Move a panel

1. Aim the reticle at the colored title bar on the desktop or camera PiP panel.
2. Press and hold the phone's touchscreen until the hold is recognized.
3. Keep holding while turning your head to move the selected panel.
4. Release the touchscreen to drop the panel.

The desktop panel can be moved horizontally and vertically within its movement
limits. The camera PiP panel can be moved independently when camera PiP is
enabled. Aim at the title bar to select a panel; aiming at the panel body does
not start a drag.

## Lens adjustments

When lens adjustments are unlocked:

- Drag horizontally with one finger to adjust the lens alignment.
- Pinch with two fingers to change lens scale.
- Enable the lens lock preference to avoid accidental lens adjustments.

Touch gestures do not move a panel; panel movement uses head movement while the
touchscreen is held.
